Delete Your Account

Welcome to the official account deletion page for UPSC CMS PYQ.

We respect your privacy and provide users with a way to request permanent deletion of their app account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

You can request deletion without reinstalling or accessing the application.

  1. Click the Request Account Deletion button below.
  2. Send the request using your registered email address.
  3. Include your registered account email in the message.
  4. Our support team may contact you for identity verification.
  5. After verification, your deletion request will be processed.
Request Account Deletion

What data will be deleted?

Once your request is verified and completed, we will delete your app account and associated personal data from our active app systems, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Data retention

Certain records may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or maintain security. Such data will be retained only for the applicable required period.
