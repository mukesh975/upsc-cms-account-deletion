Privacy Policy

App: UPSC CMS PYQ

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

UPSC CMS PYQ respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can request deletion of your account.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Name and email address provided during registration.
  • Authentication information required for password or email OTP login.
  • Subscription status and purchase-related identifiers.
  • Information required to maintain your account and registered devices.
  • Information you provide when contacting support.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Provide access to free and premium app features.
  • Manage subscriptions and verify purchases.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain app security and prevent unauthorized access.

3. Authentication and Data Storage

The app uses Supabase services for authentication and database storage. Users may sign in using email OTP verification or email and password. Authentication information is processed through the authentication service provider.

4. Payments and Subscriptions

Digital subscriptions purchased through the Android app are processed using Google Play Billing. Payment processing is handled by Google according to its terms and privacy policy.

We may process subscription status, purchase identifiers, and related records to verify entitlements and provide premium access. We do not collect or store your complete payment card details.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers such as Supabase and Google Play when necessary to provide authentication, database, subscription, and payment services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account information and restrict unauthorized access. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account through the Delete My Account feature provided by the app or through our account deletion webpage.

When an account is deleted, associated account information and subscription access are removed. Certain purchase records may be retained in anonymized form where necessary for legitimate operational or record-keeping purposes.

Account deletion page: Delete My Account

8. Children's Privacy

UPSC CMS PYQ is an educational examination preparation application. It is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
